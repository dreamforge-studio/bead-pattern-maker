FAQ

Questions, answered

How do I place an order?

Email us what you need — format, length or size, deadline, and any references. We reply with the scope, a fixed quote and a delivery date; work starts only after you approve the quote.

How are prices set?

Every service lists a starting price. The final quote depends on scope — length, complexity and turnaround — and is fixed in writing before you pay anything. It does not change mid-project unless you change the brief.

How do I pay?

Payment options are confirmed in writing in your quote email before work begins — marketplace checkout or escrow where available, or the route agreed in writing. You never pay before seeing the full quote.

What files do I receive?

It depends on the service: ready-to-post MP4 for video, high-resolution images for product sets, SVG/PDF vectors plus PNG for logos, and print-ready CMYK PDF for gift cards. The formats are listed on each service card and confirmed in your quote.

How long does delivery take — and what if I want changes?

The delivery date is part of your fixed quote. Design services include two revision rounds and corporate films three, so the first delivery is a version made for your feedback, not a final you must accept blind.

One email is enough to start — you get a fixed quote and a delivery date before you pay anything.
