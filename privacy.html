Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Bead Pattern Maker (“the Tool”) is operated by Dreamforge Studio (operated by Linkai Ren, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the Tool does — and does not — handle when you use it.

The short version

The Tool runs 100% in your browser. Your photos are processed locally on your device and are never uploaded to any server. We have no user accounts, no analytics, and no advertising trackers, and we do not collect personal data through the Tool.

Your images stay on your device

When you open a photo in the Tool, it is read and converted entirely inside your browser using your device's own processing power. The image, the generated pattern, and any files you export (PNG, CSV, PDF) never leave your device unless you choose to share them yourself.

Information stored locally on your device

The Tool stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • Your license key and active plan (Free / Pro / Studio), so the plan stays activated between visits. License keys are validated locally in the browser; they are not sent to us.
  • Basic settings needed for the Tool to work.

You can remove this data at any time by clearing your browser's site data, or by using the “Back to Free” button in the Pricing section. The Tool may also cache its own program files on your device (via a service worker) so it can work offline; these are code files only, not your data.

Payments are handled by payment platforms

Subscriptions and one-time purchases are sold and processed by third-party payment platforms — currently Gumroad, Creem, and itch.io. When you buy a plan:

  • Your payment details (such as card numbers) are entered on the platform's own checkout page and processed by that platform. We never see, receive, or store your card number or bank details.
  • The platform may share limited order information with us so we can deliver and support your purchase — typically your email address, the product purchased, and the license key issued.
  • How the platform handles your data is covered by that platform's own privacy policy.

Hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record standard technical information (such as IP addresses) in server logs when you visit, under GitHub's Privacy Statement. We do not receive or use those logs to identify visitors.

Email

If you email us for support, we will see your email address and the contents of your message, and will use them only to respond to you. We do not sell email addresses or send marketing mail.

Children

The Tool is a general-purpose craft tool and is not directed at children. Because the Tool itself collects no personal data, no personal data of children is collected through it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
