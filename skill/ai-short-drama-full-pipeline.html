What job it does, what it saves

Most AI short dramas die on drift — faces change, voices change, every clip needs patching. This trio is the exact three-step workflow behind our produced series, with a quality gate at each step: once dialogue is locked, not one character changes; each generated segment carries exactly one story beat; the voice description is copied verbatim into every segment. Start with the beginner route — one character, one scene, about 10 seconds — run the whole pipeline end to end once, then scale to a full episode (about 5 minutes).

What's inside / the method

  • Step 1 — direction & script: vertical scripts where characters actually speak; spoken lines, inner monologue and voice-over marked as three types; every episode ending passes a four-point cliffhanger check
  • Step 2 — storyboard, asset list and image/video prompts: blocking charts, spatial relations, per-line expression beats
  • Step 3 — clip-by-clip generation; repair continuity in a fixed order: edit first, patch frames second, re-generate last; edit list out
  • Six templates: character sheet, episode outline, storyboard sheet, generation log, edit list, hook-and-foreshadowing register
  • Two walkthrough cases: a 10-second beginner clip (teaching example) and a full-episode method walkthrough, plus a complete walkthrough recording script
  • One-shot complete: the three full skill texts, install guide, templates, cases and samples in one pack
