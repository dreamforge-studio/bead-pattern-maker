What job it does, what it saves

Free lite: the hard part of clipping isn't cutting, it's choosing — two hours of source, which moments are worth a short? Run all three signals, cut where all three agree first, give two-signal hits second priority, and review single-signal hits by hand before deciding. Licensing comes before everything: only clip material you own or are licensed to use. No exceptions.

What's inside / the method

  • Three-signal sweep: volume peaks, engagement heat and transcript content each mark candidate ranges
  • Intersection rule: three-signal hits first, two-signal next, single-signal only after a human look
  • Licensing red-line card stated up front
  • Vertical spec quick sheet: 9:16, a hook in the first 3 seconds, subtitle safe zones, and a human review pass on every clip before publishing
  • Free selection edition — the full workflow (transcription-based selection, vertical reframing, word-by-word captions, hook copy) is in the H3 pack

FAQ

Who is it for — and who is it not for?

For: Editors and channels cutting long videos into vertical shorts who pick moments by scrubbing timelines by hand.
Not for: Selection only — it doesn't cut or caption for you; and never for unlicensed material.

How do I use it — and what do I need first?

Get it free: run the three signals over the source, take intersections first, then cut to the vertical spec sheet (9:16, 3-second hook, subtitle safe zones) with a human review per clip.

How does it relate to the paid full version?

This is the free selection edition. The full H3 workflow pack adds transcription-based selection, vertical reframing, word-by-word captions and hook copy — the whole chain.
