What job it does, what it saves

Explainer videos fail on process, not editing: facts that were never verified go straight into the script, the visual style changes every episode, the voice is different each time, and nobody watches. This pipeline fixes the full route — every fact cross-checked against three sources, 85 points to release, and hard faults like factual errors, misaligned dubbing or subtitle typos are instant rejects.

What's inside / the method

  • Topic research and fact-checking: every fact cross-checked against three sources; anything doubtful stays out of the script
  • Script structure: a 3-second hook that opens with a question or a counter-intuitive claim → segmented breakdown → a conclusion that ends with an audience question
  • Two locked visual styles: hand-drawn notebook style for knowledge topics, realistic on-camera style for product introductions
  • One fixed voice across the whole series, plus the music and mix step
  • 100-point QC scorecard: 85 to release; factual errors, misaligned dubbing and subtitle typos are instant rejects
  • Two process walkthrough cases (method demonstrations): a hand-drawn-style concept explainer and an on-camera-style project introduction, each with the three mistakes beginners make most in that style
  • Templates: the structured script format, the QC scorecard, and the platform copy sheet. Stated plainly: this is a method and templates pack — no automated rendering code included
