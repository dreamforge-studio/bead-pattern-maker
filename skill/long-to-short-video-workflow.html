What job it does, what it saves

Everyone has the tools to cut shorts; almost nobody has a workflow that survives daily output. The main route is deliberately boring — a human-made cut-point list executed with frame accuracy, zero API keys and zero per-clip cost. Set the chain up once and a two-hour source video becomes a batch of publishable vertical shorts through one repeatable route, instead of an afternoon of scrubbing timelines by hand.

What's inside / the method

  • Main route: a human-made cut-point list executed with frame accuracy — zero API keys, zero per-clip cost
  • AI-assisted selection and face-tracking vertical crops documented as upgrade and fallback routes, with their real costs stated (one of them needs a text-model key you supply yourself before its selection step runs at all)
  • Parameter sheet on one page: cut-list format, clip duration bands, vertical specs and subtitle parameters
  • Ready-to-edit cut-list template and a segment scoring sheet for ranking and de-duplicating candidates
  • Red-line chapter first, on purpose: only clip material you own or are licensed to use; re-edit to a genuinely restructured result rather than a re-upload; human review before publishing
  • The underlying tools are free and open source; what you pay for is our tested configuration and experience
