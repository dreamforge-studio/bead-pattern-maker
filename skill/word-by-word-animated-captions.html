What job it does, what it saves

Word-by-word highlight captions are the native look of TikTok, Reels and Shorts — and typing them by hand, or renting an editing subscription just for them, is the slow way. Transcribe locally to word-level timestamps, generate animated caption files from a preset, burn them into the final video in one pass. Batch captioning becomes a script run, not an evening of dragging keyframes.

What's inside / the method

  • 27 production caption presets with complete parameters — font, size, primary and highlight colors, outline, shadow, animation type — structured to drop straight into the engine's style configuration
  • A selection table that maps content type to preset
  • One discipline rule from our own output: pick one main preset per video and keep it; style-hopping inside a single clip is the fastest way to look amateur
  • Configuration document: the minimal self-hosted chain (run the engine's backend core only, no full web stack), where word-level timestamps come from, parameter anchors, safe zones, and the frame-check acceptance pass
  • The underlying engine is free and open source (MIT) and is not redistributed — the pack points you to its official repository; what you pay for is the tuned presets and the setup route
  • Captions are for your own or licensed footage only — and when a client spec sheet governs a delivery, the client's spec wins over these presets

FAQ

Who is it for — and who is it not for?
