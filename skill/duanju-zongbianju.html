Short Drama Head Writer

Develops novels, articles, character webs or a few lines of inspiration into 1–3 minute vertical short dramas: story master plan first and confirmed, then episode outlines and single-episode deepening.

Tools Intro INTRO Intro

What job it does, what it saves

Intro page — this is the story-development layer our short-drama line reaches for first, and the upstream prototype of Step 1 in the P1 trio. The master-plan-first rule exists for one reason: a series whose direction is confirmed up front doesn't get rewritten wholesale three episodes in.

What's inside / the method

  • Story master plan first: direction is confirmed before any episode outline is written — no blind drafting
  • Single-episode micro-loop: pressure → build-up → counterattack/reversal
  • Strong end-of-episode hooks and paywall beat design
  • Genre driver library: identity contrast, hidden trump cards, face-slaps, comebacks
  • Source-extraction method: pull the main line, reversal mechanics and emotional peaks out of a novel or article
  • In our skill library at v1.3.9, continuously revised
