Fight Scene Shot Checklist Card

One cheat card for AI fight prompts: how to chain attack-and-defence causality, the five elements every action shot needs, and a fault table for soft, staged-looking fights.

What job it does, what it saves

Free lite: if your generated fights come out one-swing-and-stop, like choreography instead of combat, the prompt is missing causality and shot elements. Chain the exchange first — attack → parry → counter → transition → movement, every move answering 'what did the previous move give it?' — then fill all five shot elements before you generate.

What's inside / the method

  • Attack-and-defence causality chain quick reference: every move has a cause and a counter
  • The five shot elements: shot size, viewpoint, angle, camera move, follow target — check all five before generating
  • Rhythm quick reference for pacing the exchange
  • Soft-fight fault table: match the symptom in the output, fix that line in the prompt
  • Free cheat-sheet edition — the full design method (timed segments, coverage-angle matrix, impact and flash frames, genre recipes) is in the P4 full pack
