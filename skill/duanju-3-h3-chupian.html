AI Short Drama Storyboard & H3 Production

From a one-line story to generation-ready material segments: master plan, episode outlines with hooks and paywall beats, scripts, storyboards, and 5–15 second segments an H3 workflow runs directly.

Tools Intro INTRO Intro

What job it does, what it saves

Intro page — this is the production step where money burns fastest when it's sloppy: aspect ratio left off 9:16, a settings toggle missed, seeds changed at random. This workflow pins the order, the parameters and the seed discipline down segment by segment; it is the working prototype behind Step 3 of the P1 trio.

What's inside / the method

  • Six-step run per segment: upload reference image/audio → exclusion settings → pick the mode → paste the prompt → parameters → generate and download
  • Three modes with clear jobs: reference-to-video, first/last-frame patch shots, digital-human presenter shots
  • Seed discipline: change the seed to draw a new card, lock the seed to fine-tune, register the winning seed
  • Continuity repair for the usual failures: deformed fingers, scaling props, face swaps
  • Character voice consistency across segments, plus an edit list for the assembly step
